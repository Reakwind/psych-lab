Session 4 · הקול הפנימי

Core idea. Everyone has an inner voice. Its thoughts aren't facts; the game trains noticing a thought, holding it at a healthy distance, and choosing whether to keep it or let it pass.

Sound-off test. A little voice follows Ruth around → its thoughts are cards, some warm, some grey → a grey one taken as fact turns heavy → she steps back and chooses → harsh ones float off, kind ones warm the voice → into the game.

Anchors
The inner voice. A small cloud by Ruth's head: grey when critical, warming as she keeps kinder thoughts.
Thought cards. Same look as the Inner Voice game, with real thoughts from its deck, so the film leads straight into the game.
Arm's length. Distance, not positivity: Ruth steps back from the card before choosing.
