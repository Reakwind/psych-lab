Session 8 · איזון מחשבות

Core idea. Hard thoughts are natural, but they can light only the hard part of the picture. Balanced thinking doesn't erase the difficulty or pretend it away; it widens the view to what else is in the story, and that's a skill the brain can practise.

Sound-off test. Grey thoughts around Ruth → a picture lit only in its hard corner → Tal's family meal: too hard alone, family offers help, grey thoughts narrow the light → level scale → the light widens to the whole table, the pots still there → Ruth practises.

Anchors
