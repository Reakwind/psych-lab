Session 10 · התבוננות במחשבות

Core idea. The mind makes thoughts nonstop, and we're often inside one before we notice. Instead of being carried off, we can watch a thought, name it, and come back to the breath. That small distance lets us choose how to respond, and drifting off is part of the practice.

Sound-off test. Balloons float up from Ruth → one swallows her and the room vanishes → she watches them pass, names them, breathes → a small gap, a door opens → she drifts again (Ariel too) → notices, comes back to the room, calmer.
