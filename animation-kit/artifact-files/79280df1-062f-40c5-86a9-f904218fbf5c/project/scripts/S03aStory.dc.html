Session 3a · תרגול עם פרקינסון

Core idea. Practising with Parkinson's doesn't mean sitting still or being good at it. Practice works with whatever today brings (tremor, tiredness, a wandering mind), and each try teaches one small thing.

Sound-off test. A new guide, Ariel, meets Ruth → her doubts about "being still" → a circle of attention around her as she is, on good and bad days → postures and times to try → the mind wanders and comes back → small lessons become stones on her path.

Anchors
The circle of attention. Practice isn't sitting still: it's a soft circle around whatever is here today, tremor included.
The day line. Ease rises and falls across the day and after each pill; it shows when to try practising.
Ariel. The second guide (psychologist and mindfulness teacher), introduced on Ruth's phone the way Nira was.
How it fits the program Kept from sessions 1–2: Ruth's room, the phone entrance, the tool badges from her backpack, the path of stones at the end.New: Ariel, the mat and cushion, the day line, and the circle of attention. Session 3b and later practices reuse them.Made the same way: beats on Ariel's own pauses, one video with his voice, checked before you see it.
