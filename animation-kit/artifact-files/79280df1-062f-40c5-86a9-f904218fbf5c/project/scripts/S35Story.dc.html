מפגש 35: מיינדפולנס בחיי היום יום

Core idea. Mindfulness can be brought into ordinary moments, with flexibility and choice rather than a demand to pay attention all day.

Sound-off test. A picture of formal meditation becomes Ruth’s actual day: warm water, a drink and a familiar person. Attention visits one experience, can wander, and can return to one chosen ordinary activity.
