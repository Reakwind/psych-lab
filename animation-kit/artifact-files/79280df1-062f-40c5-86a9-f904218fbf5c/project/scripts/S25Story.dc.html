Session 25 · איך להפיק יותר מהתרגול

Core idea. Practice isn't a test to pass. Come to it without effort, let it fit your day, adapt it when something is hard, and every bit counts, even one breath.

Sound-off test. The inner voice grades Ruth's practice → the card turns into stress → she puts it aside and the circle opens → what already calms her goes into her backpack → simple tips → other anchors, options, even sleep → the card is struck out; every practice lights a stone.

Anchors
The report card. The judging mind's grades for practice, set aside early and struck out at the end.
The circle of attention. From 3a: room for whatever happens now, instead of a grade.
The path of stones. Every kind of practice, even one breath, lights a stone.
