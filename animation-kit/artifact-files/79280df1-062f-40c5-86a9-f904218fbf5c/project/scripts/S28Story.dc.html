Session 28 · מבוא לתשומת לב לנשימה ולסריקת גוף

Core idea. Attention works like a beam of light: what it lands on, we notice. Mindfulness practice trains moving it on purpose, to the breath or through the body, and gently bringing it back when it drifts, without judging or chasing a result.

Sound-off test. A beam lands on Ruth's hand and sensations appear → it drifts and she brings it back → it rests on her breath, then travels through her body → drifting, dozing, restless days are all part of it → tremor and moving too → the beam steadies.

Anchors
The beam of attention. Session 3a's circle, now a light Ruth can point: what it lands on, she feels.
The balloon. From 3a and 6: attention drifting off; noticing it and coming back is the practice.
The scan. On the mat, the beam travels through the body, or rests on the breath as an anchor.
