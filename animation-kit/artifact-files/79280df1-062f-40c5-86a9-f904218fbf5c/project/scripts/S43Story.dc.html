Session 43 · להתבונן בחוויה נעימה

Core idea. Pleasant moments are like the birds: always there, but our attention, built to spot danger, lets them slip by. Stopping to explore one pleasant moment in feeling, body and thoughts trains attention to notice them as they happen.

Sound-off test. One grey bird → birds everywhere once she is interested → they were always there → attention drifts to danger and the grey → one pleasant moment, explored: heart, body, thought → with practice the circle catches warm moments → the tree full of birds.

Anchors
The birds. Always there; seen once attention turns to them. The film opens and closes with them.
The circle of attention. From session 3a: where Ruth's attention goes, things light up.
Feeling, body, thoughts. Three places a pleasant moment shows up, lit one at a time.
