Session 1 · ברוכים הבאים

Core idea. Parkinson's is more than movement: mood, attention, motivation and thinking weigh on daily life even when nobody sees them. This program gives them space and builds small, practical tools, step by step, with Nira alongside.

Sound-off test. Ruth opens the app and meets Nira → on her path, a backpack hides four heavy stones → she sets the stones down and packs five tools instead → small steps join into a path she walks with Nira.

One world, anchors for the whole program
The path of stones = the program. The same idea as the app's Journey tab. Each session is one more stone; the last line, צעד אחר צעד, lands on it.
The backpack = what Ruth carries. Today: hidden symptoms as heavy stones. From here on: the tools she gains. Later sessions can open it again.
Cast and set. Ruth, in the living room from session 2, and Nira, the guide whose voice we hear, who appears on the phone and then walks beside her.
Carried over from session 2 Today's version: 13 parts showing 13 unrelated pictures (wave, orbit, battery, wander, tools, compass, two people, practices, fork, brain, steps, heart, landscape). Proposed: Ruth's room as home base, one path, one backpack, two people. Shots change what is on screen; the stones from Act 2 come out in Act 3 and the tools take their place. Made the same way: beats placed on Nira's own pauses in the recording, rendered as one video with her voice, and checked before you see it.

Act 3 · What we'll do here
