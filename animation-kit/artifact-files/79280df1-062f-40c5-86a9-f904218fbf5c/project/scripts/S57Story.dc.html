מפגש 57: מטאפורת המצפן

Core idea. A value can act like a compass: the direction can stay meaningful while a particular walking plan, route or size of step changes.

Sound-off test. Ruth plans a walk with a friend, then meets fatigue. The compass supplies a direction, not a mandatory destination. Seated movement, a home visit or rest become different possibilities; a winding path and the same compass make returning to a chosen direction visible.
