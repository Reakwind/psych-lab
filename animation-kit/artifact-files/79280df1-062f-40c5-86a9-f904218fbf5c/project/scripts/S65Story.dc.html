Session 65 · מטאפורת האורח הלא מוזמן

Core idea. Hard thoughts, feelings and symptoms are like an uninvited guest. Fighting to throw him out takes all our attention and energy and we miss the party. Giving him a little room, without liking or agreeing with him, lets us go back to the people and things that matter. He is in the house, but not its master.

Sound-off test. A party in Ruth's room → a loud grey guest at the door; she pushes and argues, the party goes on without her → she lets him be and goes back → he is thoughts, feelings, sensations; embarrassment, worry, tremor → fighting drains her battery and the party ends → a stool for the guest, he softens; she takes part → he sits small in the corner while she hosts.

Anchors
The party. Ruth's own living room, with friends, snacks and music. What matters to her.
The guest. A round grey figure with a frown and noise lines. He softens when given a little room, but never leaves.
The circle of attention. Where Ruth's attention is: on the door, or on her people.
