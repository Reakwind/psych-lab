Session 11 · קלפי שיחה

Core idea. The things that make us who we are (people, interests, experiences, values) get buried by the busy day. A deck of question cards opens a different conversation with ourselves and turns attention back to them; there are no right answers, you just open a card and see.

Sound-off test. Ruth turns question cards → four bright badges around her → grey blocks bury them → each card lifts a block, the badges light, her battery charges → heart, voice or notebook; the phone game; a friend joins; an action card → she opens the next card, smiling.

Anchors
The deck. Question cards, face up when a question is asked; the game itself.
Four sides of who we are. People, interests, experiences, values; buried by the day, uncovered by the cards.
The day's load. Session 2's grey effort blocks, here piled over what matters.
How it fits the program Kept: Ruth's room and armchair, the friend, the battery, the circle of attention, the big phone, the spark.Questions on cards are exactly the ones Nira reads; no answers are written for Ruth.Made the same way: beats on Nira's pauses, one video with her voice.
