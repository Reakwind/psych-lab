Session 15 · נשימת קופסה

Core idea. Box breathing: in for three, hold for three, out for three, hold for three — four equal sides of a square. A steady rhythm tells the body it can downshift and gives attention something simple to return to; it should stay easy and adjustable, never forced.

Sound-off test. The breathing badge leaves Ruth's path → a square draws beside her armchair, side by side → ragged breath becomes a steady rhythm, the meter drops, racing thoughts drift off → Ariel joins, the torso shows the body breathing on its own → two rounds: a dot travels the square, a pacer swells and settles → it melts into her natural breath → odd at first, no prize, shorten, stop if needed → the square softens: let's breathe.

Anchors
The breathing box. Four equal sides: in up the right, hold across the top, out down the left, hold along the bottom; a dot travels it on Ariel's counts.
The breathing badge. Session 14's first calm tool, waiting on Ruth's path; today it opens.
The tension meter. From sessions 3b and 14: up with ragged breath, down with a steady rhythm.
How it fits the program Kept: Ruth's room and armchair, her path with the calm badges, Ariel, session 3b's pacer and torso cutaway, the tension meter, thought balloons, the circle of attention.Only Ariel's words on screen: the four phases, the counts and the tips are his.Counts on the recording: each count and phase lands on Ariel's own pauses; the practice runs as one unbroken shot.

Act 6 · Make it yours
