Session 44 · לעשות דברים שעושים לנו טוב

Core idea. Small things that do us good are the first to be pushed out of a full day. Time for them isn't wasted; it gives air. Knowing them and giving them a little room is a skill that grows with practice, not another task.

Sound-off test. Coffee on the balcony → warm tokens on her day → grey tasks and Parkinson's push them off → a pause; air in the day → a muscle that grows → they are already there; notice what they give → one finds its place → back to the balcony.

Anchors
Ruth's day line. As in session 42: warm tokens are the things that do her good, grey blocks the demands that push them out.
The balcony. Morning coffee: the film starts and ends here.
The muscle. Ariel's image: giving good things room grows with practice.
