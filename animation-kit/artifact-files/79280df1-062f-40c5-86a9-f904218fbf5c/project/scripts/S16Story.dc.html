Session 16 · מתג המאבק

Core idea. Fighting what we feel inside switches on a second layer of difficulty (frustration, anger, more worry) that takes room and energy. We can't simply switch it off, but we can notice the click, meet ourselves kindly, and soften the struggle a little.

Sound-off test. A switch on Ruth's wall → a hard morning: tired, anxious, angry at her body → click, it's on → a stack of extra weight piles up and drains her battery → effort clears a solvable problem but makes feelings swell → it can't be switched off, it happens to everyone → she notices the click, talks to herself kindly → turns it down five percent, and has room to breathe.

Anchors
The struggle switch. A wall switch in Ruth's room: off, then on with a red light when she starts fighting what she feels.
The pile. Session 2's grey effort blocks, labelled with what Ariel names; red ones are what the switch adds.
The dimmer. No off button, but it can be turned down a little.
How it fits the program Kept: Ruth's room and armchair, the battery (session 5), thought cards and the warm inner voice (session 4), effort blocks (session 2), the path, the heart of what matters (session 9), the circle of attention, Ariel and friends.Only Ariel's words on screen: the event, the thoughts and feelings are his; Ruth's kind lines use his own wording.Made the same way: beats on Ariel's pauses; the click lands on his "קליק".
