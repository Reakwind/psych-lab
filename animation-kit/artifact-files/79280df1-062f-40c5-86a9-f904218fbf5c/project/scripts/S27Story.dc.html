Session 27 · שגרת ערב לקראת השינה

Core idea. Sleep can't be switched on, but an evening routine helps the body and head slow down gradually. Repeated each night, a few small calming steps become a signal that the day is over.

Sound-off test. Dusk; Ruth lies awake with a busy head → sleep restores, Parkinson's makes it harder → calming things light up, work and news go away → the same steps each evening → no sleep button, a gentle slope → one small sign, and rest.

Anchors
The evening room. The window goes from dusk to night and the lamp dims as the film goes on.
The gentle slope. The evening end of the day line: a calm way down from the day into sleep.
The same few steps. Shower, book, dim light, repeated each evening until they become a signal.
