Session 3b · נשימה בטנית

Core idea. Breath reflects how we feel, and it also works the other way: a slower, belly-led breath with a longer exhale can lower tension. Try it gently, adjust it to yourself, and expect nothing special.

Sound-off test. Ruth notices her breath → stress makes the wave fast, calm makes it slow → she turns it slower herself → inside, the belly rises → three paced breaths on the mat → tips, no expectations → the pacer waits for her.

Anchors
The breath wave. Fast and red under stress, slow and teal when calm; the same badge as in session 1's backpack.
The pacer. Grows for three, shrinks for five, timed to Ariel's counts; the same look as the Breathing practice that follows.
The torso cutaway. One inside view, drawn like session 2's head close-up, to show the belly rising.
