Session 66 · מבוא לחיזוק הקשב והאיזון הרגשי

Core idea. Attention often gets caught on what worries us. Like a muscle, we can train it: widen it to include more, and turn it to what matters, what is pleasant, what we appreciate. Emotional balance isn't being calm all the time: hard feelings keep their place, and other things get room too.

Sound-off test. The circle of attention drifts in Ruth's room → a grey worry grabs it and swells → she stretches and moves the circle herself → it widens over the room, then visits a photo, her tea, a new leaf → the worry drops onto a scale, warm things join the other pan and it evens → move, steady, widen → present with a friend → her phone.

Anchors
The circle of attention. From sessions 3a and 6: it gets caught, then moves, steadies and widens.
The scale. Hard feelings stay on one pan; more things join on the other.
The good things. What matters, something pleasant now, a small thing she appreciates: all in Ruth's room.
