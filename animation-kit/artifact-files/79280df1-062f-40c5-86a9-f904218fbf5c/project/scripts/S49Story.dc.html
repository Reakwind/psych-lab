מפגש 49: כמו שאני, זה טוב מספיק

Core idea. Social participation can matter and still be difficult. Ruth can choose which connections she wants to keep, make adaptations, and arrive as she is today.

Sound-off test. An invitation brings both warmth and effort. Ruth asks what draws her there and what makes it hard. She can rest or participate; when she chooses connection, symptoms do not have to disappear first. A smaller meeting or shorter visit keeps the choice practical.
