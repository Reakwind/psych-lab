Session 73 · מבוא לפעולה משמעותית

Core idea. Things that matter to us get pushed aside, all the more with Parkinson's. Stopping to ask what matters, our inner compass, points to a next step that is possible now, however small. We give ourselves credit for the effort and the choice, not grades, and each of us chooses our own direction.

Sound-off test. Warm badges of what Ruth wants → a crowded grey day pushes them aside → she stops, asks, and her compass appears → no big plan; she remembers → on her path, a possible step, sometimes tiny, on the compass line → no report card, stars for effort and choice → a friend at the door → three compasses, three ways → good memories → which direction today?
