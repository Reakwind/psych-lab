Session 76 · לשים לב → להתייצב → לבחור → לעשות צעד → לפרגן לעצמנו

Core idea. We don't remember lists of tools, but we can remember five steps that lead into each other: notice, steady, choose, take a step, acknowledge. Walking one ordinary hard moment through them shows the logic; on another day they will look different, and we deserve credit for still being here.

Sound-off test. Ruth's backpack spills many tools → too many to remember → they fold into five stones on her path → one day she doesn't want to exercise → notice the thought → steady with a breath → her compass: stay active, or do less → a small step: five minutes, easier, together → no trophy, credit for the process, warm words → another day, the five stones again → the whole path lit, be proud.
