Session 14 · מתח, חרדה והרגעת הגוף

Core idea. Stress and anxiety act on both body and mind; with Parkinson's they can also be a symptom and make other symptoms stronger. Calming tools (breathing and relaxation, grounding, mindfulness, guided imagery) ease the alarm a little; they aren't magic, and over the program we'll find which work for you.

Sound-off test.
