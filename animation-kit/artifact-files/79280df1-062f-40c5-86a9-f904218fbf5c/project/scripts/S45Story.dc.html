Session 45 · רגע של חמלה עצמית

Core idea. We find warm, supportive words easily for the people we love, and harsh ones for ourselves just when we need encouragement most. Self-compassion turns those same warm words back toward ourselves: notice it is hard, remember we are not alone, speak kindly.

Sound-off test. Ruth comforts two friends → alone and struggling, her inner voice turns grey and harsh → she pauses, notices, sees she is not alone → the warm words come back to her → on her path, harshness was extra weight; kindness lifts it → guiding sentences, some fit → the warm voice grows; 5% is a start.

Anchors
The inner voice. Session 4's little cloud: grey and harsh when Ruth struggles, warm when she turns kind.
The warm words. What Ruth says to people she loves, finally said to herself.
The path and the backpack. Parkinson's is hard enough; harshness only adds weight.
How it fits the program Kept: Ruth's room and chair, the inner voice and thought cards (4), friends (5), the circle of attention (3a), the path and backpack (1), Ariel on the phone (3), the door (7).Quotes on screen are exactly the ones Ariel says; the guiding sentences are shown as cards with lines, since their words are not in the script.Made the same way: beats on Ariel's pauses, one video with his voice.
