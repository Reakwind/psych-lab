Session 48 · תיקוף עצמי

Core idea. When things are hard we often criticise ourselves for how we feel. Self-validation recognises feelings, thoughts and sensations as legitimate and understandable in the situation, the way we would answer a crying child, without excusing every behaviour, and it helps us cope more calmly and flexibly.

Sound-off test. A hard moment and a harsh inner voice → a child's broken toy: dismissed, or given room → validating isn't excusing; it helps him be brave → "I'm a burden", answered kindly, leaves room to ask for help → three stones: identify, understand, say it → awkward at first, then it seeps in.

Anchors
The inner voice. Session 4's cloud: harsh when Ruth judges her feelings, warm when she validates them.
The boy and his broken toy. Two answers to the same tears.
Three stones. The path from session 1: identify, understand, a validating sentence.

Act 3 · The broken toy

Two answers to a crying child: dismiss it, or make room for it. Validating isn't excusing: "it makes sense you're scared, and we'll be brave". It helps him, and us, cope.
