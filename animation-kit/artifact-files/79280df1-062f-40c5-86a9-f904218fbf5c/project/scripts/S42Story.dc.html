Session 42 · מבוא ללדאוג לעצמי

Core idea. Self-care just means making sure the day holds enough of the things that lift us. It looks different for each person and each day, and with Parkinson's those things are easily squeezed out. It isn't another task, but a chance to find, step by step, what feels right.

Sound-off test. A day line fills with things → some lift (warm), some drain (grey) → the same thing differs between people and days → Parkinson's grey blocks crowd the warm ones out → she lets one go and makes room → warm ones she already has light up → step by step along the path.
