Session 32 · איך לתקשר עם אחרים על פרקינסון

Core idea. Parkinson's brings conversations Ruth didn't plan. She doesn't owe everyone an explanation: she decides whether, with whom and how much, and a few sentences prepared in advance help. With close people, a small explanation of outside versus inside clears up misunderstandings and says what help she wants.

Sound-off test. Friends arrive with questions → Ruth freezes with her own questions → the feelings are understandable → Parkinson's is her backpack, not her → she turns the dials and writes cards → over tea she uses them → a split frame shows outside versus inside → a friend helps too fast; Ruth says when and how → she holds a handful of her own words.

Anchors
Ruth's word cards. New: a small stack she prepares (shot 7); each sentence she uses becomes a card; at the end she holds a handful of her own words.
Outside / inside. A split frame: how it looks to others (right) and how it feels inside, using session 2's head cutaway and its lit bulb.
Tea with a friend. Session 12's two armchairs and tea table: where the conversations happen.
How it fits the program Kept: Ruth's room, door and armchair, friends a and b, the tea table and speech bubbles (5, 12), thought cards (4), the path and backpack (1), the head cutaway and bulb (2), the dial, hourglass, mug and tremor marks.Wording on cards: the quotes are the narration's, word for word; Ruth's boundary card uses the feminine מעדיפה (the narration says מעדיף) because she is the speaker.Made the same way: beats on Nira's pauses, one video with her voice.

Act 1 · Caught off guard

Friends drop in. One notices the tremor, one can't hear Ruth, and then the familiar "is everything okay?". Time freezes while Ruth's three questions hang over her.

Act 2 · Feelings, and a choice

Embarrassment, worry, anger, frustration: all understandable. On her path, Parkinson's is the backpack she carries, not who she is. She turns the dials (whether, with whom, how much) and writes a few cards in advance.

Shot 2.10:28–0:40

לדבר עם אנשים על פרקינסון זה לא תמיד פשוט. אצל מישהו אחד זה יעורר מבוכה או חשש , אצל מישהי אחרת כעס ותסכול.

On screenCloser on Ruth: four feelings come up around her, two and two, as her face changes: מבוכה · חשש, then כעס · תסכול. Tag: לא תמיד פשוט. Carries overSame room. TransitionA tag on each word.
Shot 2.20:40–0:45

התחושות האלה מובנות ולא תמיד קל למצוא את המילים בזמן אמת.

On screenA warm glow settles on her (התחושות האלה מובנות); a speech bubble tries to form but holds only jumbled marks: לא תמיד קל למצוא את המילים בזמן אמת. Carries overWarm glow (5–7). TransitionBubble fizzles.
Shot 2.30:45–0:56

Act 4 · Outside and inside

With close people the frame splits: how it looks from outside and how it feels inside (session 2's head cutaway). Slowness is not laziness; a still face is not lack of interest. Small sentences clear it up and say what helps.

Shot 4.11:28–1:42

לפעמים דווקא עם האנשים הקרובים, שמכירים אתכם הכי טוב, צריך להסביר יותר. כי חלק מהסימפטומים יכולים להיראות מבחוץ אחרת לגמרי ממה שהם מרגישים מבפנים.

On screenThe frame splits: מבחוץ (right) — Ruth as her friend sees her; מבפנים (left) — the head cutaway from session 2. עם הקרובים, צריך להסביר יותר. Carries overHead cutaway, spark-maker bulb (2). TransitionSplit wipes open on "מבחוץ".
Shot 4.21:42–1:52

Act 6 · Finding the words

Not knowing what to say is fine. Little by little Ruth's stack of cards grows into a handful of her own words: understood, boundaries kept, support her way.

Shot 6.12:53–3:10

לא תמיד קל לדבר על הדברים האלה, ולא צריך לדעת מראש מה לומר בכל מצב. לאט לאט אפשר למצוא את המילים שעוזרות לכם להרגיש מובנים ומובנות יותר, לשמור על הגבולות שלכם ולקבל תמיכה בדרך שמתאימה לכם.

On screenRuth fans out her handful of cards, both friends beside her; three warm tags: להרגיש מובנים · לשמור על הגבולות · לקבל תמיכה. Opening tag: לאט לאט אפשר למצוא את המילים. Carries overCards, friends, the room. TransitionCards fan out; tags on their words.
