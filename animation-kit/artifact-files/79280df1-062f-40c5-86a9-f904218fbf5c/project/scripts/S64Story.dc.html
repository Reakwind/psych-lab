Session 64 · טיפוח חשיבה גמישה

Core idea. We can't control which thoughts come, and we don't have to erase, argue with or fix them. We can notice when one gets too loud — too many exclamation marks, like orders — and that moment lets us pause, step back and choose how to respond. That is mental flexibility, and it can be trained.

Sound-off test. Small thoughts drift by → one swells into a heavy, shouting card and drags Ruth down → thoughts come to everyone; no erasing or arguing → she notices the exclamation marks, pauses, labels it, steps back, chooses → a stretch and a bending reed → four tool badges → her phone: which fits today?
