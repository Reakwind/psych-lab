Session 29 · תשומת לב לנשימה וסריקת גוף

Core idea. Attention works like a beam: the sensations were always there, and what changes is where we point it. In the two practices Ruth chooses where it goes, to the breath or through the body, and every time it drifts (to dinner), noticing and coming back is the practice itself. No result to reach.

Sound-off test. A beam lands on Ruth's hand and sensations appear → it hops around until she takes hold of it → it watches her breath, then travels through her body → it drifts to dinner, a spark as she notices, and back, again and again → sunny or cloudy, both are fine → she shifts, settles, and gives herself a few minutes.

Anchors
The beam of attention. From session 28: what it lands on, Ruth notices. This time she learns to choose where it goes.
The balloon on a string. Attention drifting (to dinner); noticing and reeling it back is the practice.
Day cards. From 3a: calm one time, tired another. Both count; there is no result to reach.
How it fits the program Kept: Ruth's room, armchair and mat, the beam and hand close-up (28), the balloon (3a), the breath wave and torso cutaway (3b), postures and day cards (3a), the spark (2), Ariel on the phone (3a).Not repeating 28: the new beats are choosing where the beam goes, the breath cutaway, the dinner balloon and the row of returns.Made the same way: beats on Ariel's pauses, one video with his voice.
