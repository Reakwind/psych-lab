מפגש 37: מטאפורת השחמט

Core idea. Thoughts and feelings are pieces on a chessboard. Ruth can make room for all the pieces without having to make one side win, and still choose a meaningful response.

Sound-off test. An inner argument uses energy. Opposing pieces appear on one board and move in battle. The whole board then becomes visible; the pieces can stay while attention widens and Ruth chooses where to go next.
