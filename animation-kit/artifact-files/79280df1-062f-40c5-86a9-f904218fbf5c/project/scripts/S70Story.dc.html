Session 70 · להרגיע את הגוף

Core idea. Our bodies often hold tension without our noticing: a furrowed brow, a clenched jaw, a held breath. Noticing it and helping the body let some of it go can help how we feel and function. Simple everyday things can help; the aim isn't total calm, just a lower gear, and with practice it comes more easily.

Sound-off test. Ruth's brow and jaw tighten, then a big held breath → the tension meter rises with stress, symptoms, a hard day → stiff, she struggles to water the plant → a scan finds the tension, she lets some go, the water flows → five everyday helpers → each person a different one → not zero, a lower gear → a break, recovery, sleep → with practice it comes easier → her phone: which one?

Act 3 · Notice and let go

A soft scan line finds the tension; she lets some of it go, the meter drops a notch, and the watering flows. With time the body learns to do it by itself.

Shot 3.10:30–0:43
