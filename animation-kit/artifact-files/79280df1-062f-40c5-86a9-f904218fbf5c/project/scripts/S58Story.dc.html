Session 58 · רגע, זה חשוב לי

Core idea. Things that matter don't always get room in a busy day. Picking one, asking four short questions about it, and finding one small, possible step gives it a little more room.

Sound-off test. Ruth's day is packed with grey blocks and a warm heart is left out → five things that matter, she picks one → four question cards → blocks shift and a warm stone fits in → three small steps → she steps onto the first stone.

Anchors
The full day. Grey task blocks edge to edge; what matters is left outside until she makes room.
The badge of what matters. Ruth picks one (people) and the four questions turn around it.
A warm stone. The small step is one stone on her path: reachable, possible.
How it fits the program Kept: Ruth's room and path of stones (session 1), the day running right to left (3a), the spark (session 2), the phone and app.Words on screen are only Ariel's: the five things that matter, the four questions, the three example steps.Made the same way: beats on Ariel's pauses, one video with his voice.
