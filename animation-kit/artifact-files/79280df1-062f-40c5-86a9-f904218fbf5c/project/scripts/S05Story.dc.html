Session 5 · ניהול אנרגיה ואנלוגיית הבטריה

Core idea. Energy works like a battery that drains and charges and starts every day at a different level. Pacing means checking it now and then and adjusting, flexibly, so there's enough left for what matters.

Sound-off test. A battery beside Ruth → it rises and falls through the day → things drain or charge it → she checks it and adapts the task → keeps a reserve, spends more for what matters → stops today to check.
