Session 17 · הרפיה ודמיון מודרך

Core idea. Relaxing can be learned: not by giving the body orders, but by giving it conditions. Relaxation and guided imagery work because brain and body respond to what we imagine as well as to what happens; with practice the way from tension to rest grows familiar.

Sound-off test. Orders bounce off a tense Ruth → she waters her plant and it grows → two calm badges and what they help with → a scan eases her body, a bubble opens on a pleasant place → in the head, imagined and real light alike → a stormy thought tenses her, a calm one eases her → her path grows familiar → any posture, no statue, no perfect picture, drift and return → a small, kind pause.

Anchors
Conditions, not orders. Ruth's plant grows when watered; the body relaxes when given the conditions.
The imagination bubble. A pleasant place (her own hills); grey and stormy when a stressful thought comes.
The tension meter. From sessions 3b and 14: up with orders and scary thoughts, down with conditions and calm images.
How it fits the program Kept: Ruth's room, plant, armchair and mat, the tension meter, the calm badges (session 14), the head cutaway (session 2), her path of stones, the balloon on a string (session 10), Ariel on her phone.Only Ariel's words on screen: the orders, the uses, the ways to practise are his; the pleasant place is left as her own hills.Made the same way: beats on Ariel's pauses, one video with his voice.

Act 6 · How we'll practise

Get comfortable any way; no need to be a statue; no need for a clear image; whatever comes is fine; drifting off and coming back is part of it.
