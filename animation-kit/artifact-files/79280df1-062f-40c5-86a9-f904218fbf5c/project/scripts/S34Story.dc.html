מפגש 34: הדב הלבן

Core idea. Trying to force an unwanted thought away can make it take more attention. Ruth explores a second dial: willingness, while the white bear can stay without choosing her actions.

Sound-off test. A white bear appears when Ruth tries not to think of it. Attempts to push it away bring it back. Two radio dials distinguish intensity from willingness; Ruth turns toward the rest of her life while the bear remains smaller beside her.

Act 3 · Two different dials

A radio with two separate knobs appears; the bear stays above it. The first knob represents intensity; its position changes while the bear is still present. The desired reduction does not simply remove the bear; the first knob is not presented as a direct control. Attention shifts to the second knob, willingness; the bear does not need to vanish. The same bear and feeling can be unpleasant without turning willingness into approval.
