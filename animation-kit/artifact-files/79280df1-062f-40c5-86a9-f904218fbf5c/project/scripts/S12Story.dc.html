Session 12 · לשתף מישהו קרוב בדרך

Core idea. People close to us see a lot but can't know how it feels inside or what we need. Talking about what helps, what less and what we'd like to try, and hearing what they can give, turns support into something found together; the choice is ours, and one small share can be enough to start.

Sound-off test. Ruth walks her path, a friend beside her who can't see inside the backpack → the same reminder helps or presses → they talk over tea → a dial for how much → one small step → things from the backpack to share → breathe, move, walk together → one person, one thing → stones light as they walk on.
