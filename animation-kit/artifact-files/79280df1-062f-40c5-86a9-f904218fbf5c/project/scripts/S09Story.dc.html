Session 9 · מטאפורת נהג האוטובוס

Core idea. Life is a bus heading toward what matters. Thoughts, feelings, memories and symptoms ride along as passengers, some loud. Fighting them lets them steer; we can keep our hands on the wheel, notice who is talking, and choose the next turn.

Sound-off test. Ruth drives toward a heart → passengers climb aboard, some shouting → she turns round to argue, the bus veers off and her battery drains → she faces forward, hands on the wheel → a light on who's talking, a breath, warm passengers too → she takes the turn toward the heart.
