מפגש 53: למצוא את הדרך שלכם להישאר מחוברים

Core idea. A conversation can need more time and small adaptations. Ruth tries ways to stay connected that fit her communication today.

Sound-off test. A fast-moving conversation resembles joining a busy road. The pace then slows: time, clarification, a quieter one-to-one setting and preparation become options. A familiar person waits and the connection remains.
