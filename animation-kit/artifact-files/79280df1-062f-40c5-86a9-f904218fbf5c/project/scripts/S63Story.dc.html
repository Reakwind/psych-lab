Session 63 · להבין את מעגל ההתמודדות עם קושי

Core idea. A difficult moment is more than what happens in the body. A symptom, a thought, attention, feelings and tension, and what we do all feed each other in a cycle. The same cycle can turn the other way: a small change in one part helps the others.

Sound-off test. Ruth at a café, her hand shakes → a harsh thought, a spotlight, she shrinks back and later avoids → the moment becomes a ring of five parts that keeps turning → one part warms and the ring turns back → small changes at the café and at home → tools on each part → "what could help here?"

Anchors
The café. One everyday moment that opens and closes the film.
The cycle. Five parts in Nira's order; arrows round it and across it.
The tool badges. From the backpack (session 1): each part of the cycle has tools.
