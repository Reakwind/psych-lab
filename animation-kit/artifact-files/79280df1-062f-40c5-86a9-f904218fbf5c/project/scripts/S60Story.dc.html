Session 60 · שינויים בתפקידים שלנו

Core idea. Our roles are part of who we are. Parkinson's can change how we fill them, and that can hurt. But the role and the way we fill it are different things: the way can take another shape, with the people around us, while we stay connected to what matters in it. And some losses are simply real.

Sound-off test. A ring of role badges around Ruth → two dim, the keys pass, it hurts → a card: the role on top, the way below → she navigates instead of driving, a shorter quieter visit, guiding others → talking with family about help → one badge put away → the threads still hold.
