Session 6 · מבוא לקרקוע

Core idea. When the mind is somewhere else, grounding brings some attention back to what is right here, through the body, the senses and the room. It needs nothing special, works with tremor and tiredness, and any moment is a good one to practise.

Sound-off test. Ruth's attention floats off like a balloon → the string brings it back, roots at her feet → the chair, her feet, sounds light up → tremor is part of it → tense or happy moments, a minute at a time → into the practice.

Anchors
The balloon on a string. Attention drifting off; grounding draws it gently back.
Roots. Small roots from her feet: connecting to the ground, through body, senses and surroundings.
Lighting up. Ordinary things already here (chair, feet, sounds) glow as she notices them.
How it fits the program Kept: Ruth's room, Ariel's voice, the balloon and the circle of attention from 3a, the day line.New: roots and the glow of noticing; the grounding practice that follows can use both.Made the same way: beats on Ariel's pauses, one video with his voice.
