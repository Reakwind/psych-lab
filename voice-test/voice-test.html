What changed in B and C

  • Real pauses. Each sentence is generated on its own with a set pause after it: longer after a question or “...”, and longest in the breathing practice.
  • Pace by type. Explainer videos are 8% slower than normal speech; the breathing practice is 14% slower and slightly lower.
  • Counts on a beat. In box breathing every count lands exactly 1.25 seconds apart, so each phase lasts 3.75 seconds.
  • Text fixes. “ל3” is read as “לשלוש”, “ACT” as “אקט”, and quote marks are no longer read as pauses.
  • Sound. Gentle EQ (a little more body, less harshness and hiss), light compression, and the same loudness across files.
