Psych Lab

Phone preview

Press Play on the phone to walk any of the 87 psychology sessions as core.mjs sequences them from program.json: intro pages, films, choices, breathing, recordings, Inner Voice, Cardversation and reflections.

App prototype · below

The App prototype board lives the program as Ruth would, one app day at a time, in Hebrew: Today offers the next session not yet done (missed days move the rest later; owner decision 2026-11-03), Journey lists all 87, and the Library gathers the breathing, recordings, games and reflections met so far, to repeat without their intro film. Lab controls beside it move the calendar, miss days and jump ahead; after session 87 it follows the spec's suggested 7-day rhythm. It plays the same sessions and step screens as the phone above.

Its program text is quoted from the sources; its Hebrew interface words (tabs, buttons, status words) quote the original design, as the owner approved, and are listed with their files in canvas/interface-he.json.

What the phone shows

Hebrew only where a program source has it. Interface words are English Lab labels. Screens whose program text is in no source (motivation, post-practice question, in-practice breathing prompts, choice heading, missing Inner Voice feedback, closing message) show No source text.

Breathing phase names and timings are Lab assumptions. Missing recordings and videos keep their time slot and say so. Sessions 8 and 28 play the current Remepy app clips PS-CONT-029 and PS-CONT-013; PS-CONT-013 is a 720p preview encode.

Timing

The panel beside the phone measures visible time per session and per step, compares the content plan with the 30-minute planned day (Protocol.xlsx light or heavy speech plan), and keeps a log of timed runs in this browser. A run counts as a complete experience only when nothing was skipped, scrubbed, missing or ended early.
